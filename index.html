My Journey

Blurring the Lines Between IT and Industry

As a student in the Excellence Program (PFIEV), I realized early on that the true power of technology lies in combining the flexibility of software with the robustness of hardware. Majoring in Industrial Informatics has given me a comprehensive perspective on automation and system integration.

"Industrial Informatics Engineer | Coding with logic, Building with passion."
